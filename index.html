FAQs

Everything You Need To Know About Our Solar Solutions

What services does ConfiEra PowerTech provide?

We provide end-to-end Solar EPC solutions including Residential, Commercial, Industrial, and Institutional rooftop solar, Ground-Mounted solar, Net Metering liaisoning, and Operation & Maintenance (O&M).

How much can I save on electricity by switching to solar?

Solar typically reduces electricity bills by up to 70% to 90%. For example, a 10 kWp residential setup saves over ₹1.1 Lakhs annually, while a 100 kWp industrial setup saves ₹11+ Lakhs annually.

What is Net Metering and do you help with government approvals?

Net metering allows you to export surplus generated power back to the grid for utility credits. ConfiEra handles complete documentation, discom (e.g. MSEDCL) approvals, and bidirectional meter testing/installation.

Can ConfiEra handle large commercial & industrial solar projects?

Yes. With over 17 MW of executed capacity and 700+ installations across Maharashtra, our engineering team specializes in large-scale rooftop and ground-mounted industrial EPC projects.

What warranties and quality standards do you offer?

We only use Tier-1 solar modules backed by a 25-year performance warranty, top-tier string inverters with 5-10 year warranties, and provide dedicated workmanship warranties and O&M packages.

How do I get started with a site survey?

Simply get in touch via our contact form or call us at +91 9156171235. Our solar engineers will review your past electricity bills and conduct a detailed on-site technical survey.
