Plant Asset Care & Preventative Maintenance

Solar Operations, Maintenance (O&M) & AMC Services

Protect your solar capital investment, eliminate soiling losses, prevent hotspot failures, and ensure peak kilowatt-hour generation throughout the 25-year plant lifespan.

Dust deposition (soiling), loose cable terminations, micro-cracks, and inverter thermal throttling can degrade solar plant output by 15% to 30% if left unmanaged. Routine professional maintenance is critical to achieving expected return on investment and maintaining manufacturer warranty compliance.

ConfiEra PowerTech Solutions Pvt Ltd provides structured Comprehensive and Non-Comprehensive O&M / AMC packages for residential societies, commercial rooftops, and MW-scale industrial plants. Our specialized technicians utilize calibrated drone thermal imaging, robotic module cleaners, I-V curve tracers, and 24/7 cloud SCADA monitoring to guarantee peak generation efficiency.

Core O&M Deliverables

Thermal Thermography & Hotspot Audits

Infrared thermographic scanning to detect micro-cracks, bypass diode failures, and hidden hotspots before they cause panel degradation or fire risk.

High-Pressure & Robotic Cleaning

TDS-controlled demineralized water cleaning cycles and waterless robotic scrubbing options that preserve anti-reflective coatings.

Inverter & Switchgear Diagnostics

Comprehensive testing of inverters, AC/DC combiner boxes, MCBs, SPDs, chemical earthing resistance (below 1 ohm), and transformer oil.

Monthly PR Analytics & Billing Audits

Detailed Performance Ratio (PR) reports, Generation-vs-Insolation analytics, and DISCOM net metering credit verification.

AMC Service Packages Comparison

Routine Module Cleaning Bi-weekly / Monthly scheduled cleaning using filtered pH-neutral water or robotic crawlers
Preventative Electrical Check Quarterly torque testing of electrical lugs, tightening of clamps, and insulation resistance (Megger)
Thermography & Hotspot Inspection Bi-annual calibrated FLIR radiometric thermal imaging audit with detailed defect reports
Earthing & Lightning Verification Yearly Earth Pit resistance testing and chemical re-filling to guarantee < 1 Ohm resistance
Emergency Breakdown Response Guaranteed 4 to 24-hour technician on-site response time with spare parts inventory buffer
Net Metering & DISCOM Support Assistance with utility billing reconciliation, banking credit verification, and meter recalibration

Preventative Maintenance Workflow

1
Comprehensive Health Audit (Day 0)

Initial baseline testing of all solar strings, open-circuit voltage (Voc), short-circuit current (Isc), and inverter efficiency.

2
Automated Cloud SCADA Setup

Integration with ConfiEra's centralized monitoring desk for real-time yield tracking and automated anomaly alarms.

3
Scheduled Field Maintenance Cycles

Execution of scheduled module washing, mechanical structure retightening, and electrical balance-of-system inspection.

4
Monthly Generation & Savings Certification

Delivery of monthly generation certificates, degradation trendlines, and DISCOM credit tracking reports.
