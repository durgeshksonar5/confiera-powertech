Megawatt Scale & HT Synchronization

Heavy Industrial Energy Transition

Industrial Solar EPC for Manufacturing Plants & Sheds

Power heavy continuous factory loads, maximize power factor stability, and insulate your industrial bottom line from surging high-tension (HT) grid power costs.

Industrial factories across automotive, textile, chemical, engineering, plastics, and food processing sectors require massive, continuous electrical loads. With grid industrial tariffs escalating each year, electrical utility bills directly impair product pricing and manufacturing competitiveness.

ConfiEra PowerTech Solutions Pvt Ltd specializes in heavy-duty industrial rooftop and shed solar installations ranging from 100 kW to multiple Megawatts (MW). Our engineering teams manage high-voltage HT substation step-up, transformer synchronization, harmonic mitigation, lightning protection, and zero-penetration industrial sheet mounting without stopping your factory production line for a single minute.

Industrial EPC Capabilities

HT 11kV / 22kV / 33kV Grid Sync

Comprehensive high-tension transformer integration, vacuum circuit breakers (VCB), and relay coordination certified by CEIG.

Zero Shed Leakage Guarantees

Specialized aluminum non-penetrative clamps engineered for curved, trapezoidal, and standing seam industrial roofs.

Hazardous Area Safety Standards

Explosion-proof enclosures, fire safety compliance, and dual lightning protection suitable for industrial zones.

Continuous Production Uptime

Installation executed during planned shift windows with zero disruption to active manufacturing lines and automated machinery.

Industrial Specifications & Electrical Scope

Typical Plant Scale 100 kWp to 5 MW+ (Factory Sheds, Warehouses, Logistics Hubs)
PV Module Tier High-Wattage TOPCon / Mono PERC Glass-to-Glass Bifacial (580Wp - 620Wp)
Inverter Class Heavy-duty Central / High-Capacity String Inverters (100kW - 330kW units, IP66 rated)
Structure Engineering STAAD.Pro wind load certified aluminum alloy / Pre-galvanized structures (160+ km/h rating)
Grid Integration 11kV / 22kV / 33kV HT Step-Up Compact Substation (CSS) with HT metering & CEIG clearance
SCADA & Telemetry Industrial Modbus / IEC 60870-5-104 SCADA with meteorological station and string monitoring

Industrial Project Execution Lifecycle

1
Structural Integrity & Purlin Testing

Thorough structural testing of industrial shed purlins, dead-weight capacity, and wind uplift factors using STAAD.Pro simulation.

2
Electrical SLD & CEIG Engineering

Comprehensive Single Line Diagrams (SLD), short-circuit fault analysis, transformer protection, and statutory Electrical Inspector approvals.

3
Industrial EPC Execution

Stringent adherence to industrial HSE protocols, tool-box talks, certified lifelines, and safety netting during rooftop erection.

4
HT Synchronization & Performance Ratio Testing

Commissioning under CEIG supervision, net metering synchronization, and rigorous Performance Ratio (PR) validation above 80%.
