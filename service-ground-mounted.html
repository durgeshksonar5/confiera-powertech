Utility & Captive Open-Land Solar Farms

Utility & Captive Ground-Mounted Solar Farms

Monetize unutilized barren land, build high-yield captive open-access solar power farms, and supply high-tension energy directly to your remote manufacturing plants.

When rooftop space is insufficient to meet your total electrical power demand, ground-mounted solar farms offer virtually unlimited scalability. Through Group Captive and Open Access models, enterprises can generate clean electricity in high-irradiance rural lands and wheel power across the Maharashtra state grid (MSETCL / MSEDCL) directly to their urban factories.

ConfiEra PowerTech Solutions Pvt Ltd delivers comprehensive EPC for ground-mounted solar projects from 500 kW to multi-megawatt solar parks. Our services cover topographical surveying, geotechnical soil pull-out testing, piling foundations, high-voltage transmission lines, substation construction, and long-term regulatory compliance.

Engineering & Commercial Advantages

Group Captive & Open Access

Save up to 45% on power tariffs by wheeling solar energy from off-site solar farms with cross-subsidy surcharge exemptions.

Ramming & Piling Foundations

Customized soil-specific foundations including concrete bore piling and direct hydraulic ramming for extreme terrain stability.

Single-Axis Tracker Options

Optional single-axis astronomical solar tracking integration delivering 15% to 22% higher energy generation compared to fixed tilt.

Transmission Bay & SLDC Approvals

Turnkey transmission line erection, pooling substation bay construction, and State Load Despatch Centre (SLDC) telemetry sync.

Ground-Mounted Technical Specifications

Project Scale & Land 500 kW to 25 MW+ (~3.5 to 4 Acres per Megawatt)
Solar PV Technology Bifacial N-Type TOPCon / Dual Glass Modules (600Wp - 700Wp+ utility grade)
Mounting Architecture Seasonal Tilt Ground Structure / Single-Axis Horizontal Tracker with 550 GSM HDG coating
Inverter Architecture Outdoor Central Inverters / High-Power String Inverters with Skid-Mounted Transformer Stations
Evacuation Voltage 11 kV / 22 kV / 33 kV / 132 kV dedicated transmission line to nearest MSETCL substation
Safety & Security Perimeter solar fencing, IP CCTV surveillance, automated robotic dry-cleaning compatibility

Turnkey Ground EPC Lifecycle

1
Land Survey & Evacuation Feasibility

Drone contour mapping, geotechnical soil testing, flood level analysis, and DISCOM substation capacity validation.

2
Civil Grading & Foundation Piling

Land leveling, drainage channels, automated pile driving, and high-tensile hot-dip galvanized structure erection.

3
Substation & Transmission Line Erection

Step-up transformers, switchyard breakers, HT cabling, transmission towers, and SCADA control room construction.

4
Grid Charging & Commercial Operation (COD)

Chief Electrical Inspector (CEIG) clearance, SLDC tele-metering sync, and official Commercial Operation Date (COD) declaration.
