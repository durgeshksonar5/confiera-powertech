Commercial Energy Optimization

Commercial Solar Systems for Offices, IT Parks & Retail

Cut peak daytime tariff rates, hedge against future utility tariff hikes, and achieve ESG sustainability goals with high-output commercial solar EPC.

Commercial establishments face some of the highest grid electricity tariffs in India (₹10 – ₹16 per unit). For IT parks, corporate towers, shopping malls, private hospitals, hotels, and cold storage warehouses, energy costs represent up to 25-40% of total operational expenditure.

ConfiEra PowerTech Solutions engineers tailored commercial solar plants configured for maximum daytime generation when HVAC, lighting, and computing servers run at peak load. By integrating rooftop PV with existing diesel generators (DG-PV synchronization) and smart net metering, your business locks in levelized electricity costs under ₹3 per unit for the next 25 years.

Why Commercial Solar is a Smart Business Decision

40% Accelerated Depreciation

Claim 40% tax depreciation in the first year under Section 32 of the Income Tax Act, drastically reducing your corporate tax liability.

Zero-Export & DG Sync Controls

Intelligent controllers prevent reverse power flow into backup generators while optimizing diesel fuel savings up to 60%.

ESG & Carbon Offset Compliance

Enhance green building ratings (IGBC / LEED) and demonstrate quantifiable decarbonization to investors and stakeholders.

High Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

Deliver internal rate of return (IRR) between 28% and 35% with total project payback achieved within 3 to 3.5 years.

Technical Architecture & Engineering Scope

Typical Plant Capacity 20 kW to 500 kW+ (Rooftop / Carport / Metal Shed)
Solar PV Technology Bifacial N-Type TOPCon Modules (570Wp - 600Wp, High Temperature Coefficient)
Inverter Technology Multi-MPPT Three-Phase Commercial String Inverters with Remote I-V Curve Diagnostic
Roof Compatibility RCC Flat Roofs, Klip-Lok Metal Sheet Clamp (Non-Penetrative), and Solar Carport Structures
Smart Monitoring SCADA / IoT-enabled cloud portal with real-time solar yield, PR ratio, and fault detection alerts
Liaisoning & Compliance Complete Electrical Inspector (CEIG) approval, HT/LT net metering sanction, and DISCOM sign-off
